# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

**pocket-ledger** is a personal finance desktop application built and used by a single individual (the "owner") for private, non-commercial purposes. It is not offered to the public.

## What data is accessed

- With the owner's explicit consent, the application connects through **Enable Banking** (a PSD2 account information service) to the owner's **own** bank accounts.
- Access is **read-only**: account details, balances and transactions. The application cannot initiate payments or modify accounts.

## Where data is stored

- All financial data is stored **locally on the owner's device** in an encrypted local database.
- Access tokens are stored in the operating system's secure credential store.
- Bank login credentials are **never** seen, entered into, or stored by the application. Authentication happens directly on the bank's website.

## What is not done

- No data is shared with, sold to, or transferred to any third party.
- No analytics, telemetry, advertising or tracking of any kind.
- No data is uploaded to any server operated by the owner or anyone else, other than the requests to Enable Banking required to retrieve the owner's own account data.

## Consent and revocation

Consent can be revoked at any time, either from the bank (Openbank) or from Enable Banking. Once revoked, the application can no longer retrieve data. Locally stored data can be deleted by the owner at any time by removing the application's data folder.
